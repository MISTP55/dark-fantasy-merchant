# City view

## The grid

- `CityGrid` (Core) is a rectangle of cells; a cell is a `Vector2Int` with x to the right and y up, (0, 0) at the smallest corner. Some cells are forbidden (water, rock, fixed scenery): nothing can ever cover them.
- A `Footprint` is given once, in its `Along` orientation (`Width` on x, `Depth` on y); `Across` swaps the two. `GridPlace.Origin` is the covered cell of smallest x and y, whatever the orientation. A square footprint has one orientation: `GetPlaces` lists it `Along` only.
- `CanPlace` refuses a footprint with a side under 1 (`default(Footprint)` is 0 by 0) and `Place` throws for it: it would give room with no place.
- The grid only knows footprints, kinds and owners; it never knows who builds, what it costs or why. Rules about that (money, materials, class housing) stay in the callers.
- `BuildingKind.HousedClass` throws for a workshop and `Good` for a house: test `IsHouse` first.
- `GetPlaces` lists by x then y, `Along` before `Across`; callers that pick "the first place" rely on that order.

## Roads and the town hall

- The road network is kept by the grid, not recomputed by callers: a road is a 1 by 1 building, the town hall is the grid's `TownHall`, and a road is a network road (`IsNetworkRoad`) when joined to the town hall from touching cell to touching cell. The town hall alone connects nothing: a house or workshop is connected when it touches a network road.
- What touches is the grid's `Contact` (`RoadContact`), given at its creation from `BuildingsDefinition.RoadContact` and never changed: by a side or a corner, or by a side only. Every neighbour test of the network, of `RoadPlanner` and of `RoadsidePlacement` goes through `CityGrid.Touches`; a new one must too, or the two contacts disagree. The layouts are connected by sides, so that they work under either contact (`CityViewContentTests` checks both).
- `PlaceRoads` floods the network once for a whole trace; placing roads one by one floods once each. Use it for any group of roads.
- `BuildingConnected` is the only way room and hiring start. `BuildingPlaced` is raised before the flood: a `BuildingPlaced` handler must not read `IsNetworkRoad` or connection state; refresh on `BuildingConnected` (once per house or workshop, after the `BuildingPlaced` that caused it).
- `RoadPlanner` finds the fewest free cells that make a building touch the network (`TryFindRoads`), and `Connects` checks an already chosen set of roads: anything that places a building together with its roads checks `Connects` before taking anything. `RoadsidePlacement` is the strategy in use (the places needing the fewest roads, at random among them); `RandomPlacement` is kept but unused, since it ignores roads and would leave houses unconnected.
- A layout without a town hall is unusable (`TryCreateGrid` fails, the city gets no grid). The layout window can add one and the roads connecting its starting houses; the existing layouts were migrated once with the layout window's "Town hall and roads" (`CityLayoutEditing.AutoConnect`), which must be run on any later layout without a town hall. It changes nothing, and counts every starting house as unconnected, when the layout's content cannot stand (overlaps).

## Projection

- `IsoProjection` (Core): cell (0, 0) is at the bottom of the screen, x goes to the upper right and y to the upper left, so that the grid as the layout window shows it (x east, y north) appears turned, not mirrored: its north at the upper left, its east at the upper right. The nearer of two blocks is the one beyond the other towards smaller x or y. `CellAt` is the exact inverse of `CenterOf` on every point of a cell's diamond. `DrawKeyOf` orders buildings for drawing: a larger key is drawn later. It is a `long` and not a sortingOrder: the city view ranks the placed buildings by it and gives each sprite its rank; ground cells sit below all buildings. The key is lexicographic, the centre (`2 * (origin.x + origin.y) + size.x + size.y`, smaller drawn later, not the far cell, which ties for neighbours of different sizes) then an unclamped tie-break on the far corner's side of the diagonal (same-shape neighbours tie on the centre); it must not be clamped or squeezed into a short (a staircase of same-shape blocks needs ~400 tie-break values). Good up to 1000 by 1000. `DrawKeyOf_OrdersEveryFrontBlockAfterTheOneBehindIt_*` brute-force 1×1, 2×1, 1×2, 2×2, 3×2 and 2×3 blocks (origins 0..19 and the far corners of a 200×200 grid); a new footprint size needs them extended.
- `PlacementCursor` (Core): the footprint being placed; `Start` always begins `Along`, `Rotate` does nothing when not placing or for a square footprint.

## The market on its grid

- A class's room is its connected houses on the grid × the room of a house of the class; never `AddRoom` outside a placed house when the market has a grid. `CityMarket` listens to `CityGrid.BuildingConnected`: a house gives its room when it connects (at once when it touches a network road), and keeps it waiting until then (the player's: the room it was built with; the city's and the starting ones, connected later: its class's housing rule, which a grid holding an unconnected house must have). Only the houses the market places, or that are on the grid at its creation, give room: a house placed on the grid directly afterwards gives none. A player's workshop on the grid hires only while connected. The city's houses (`CityMarket.BuildCityHouse`, materials checked, then a place and its roads found and checked with `RoadPlanner.Connects`, then taken: the house comes with its roads and is not built when no place can be connected) and the player's (`Construction.TryBuildHouse`/`TryBuildWorkshop` with a place, checked before anything is paid) are the only ways to build.
- A market with a grid needs a placement strategy and a footprint on every housing rule (`CityHousingRule.Footprint` is nullable, required only then); without a grid the placeless `AddPlayerHouse`/`AddPlayerWorkshop` apply, and with one they throw.

## Grid data (Game)

- A building asset has a `footprint` (Along: x = width, y = depth), each side at least 1 (`TryValidate`); the scene setup fills an unset (0, 0) one: 2 by 1 for houses, 2 by 2 for workshops. Never build a `Footprint` from it without validating (`default(Footprint)` is 0 by 0 and skips validation).
- `BuildingsDefinition.PlacementStrategy` (a `PlacementStrategyDefinition`, `Assets/Data/Buildings/Placement/`) is required: `WorldEconomy` makes one strategy for the whole world from it. The scene setup gives the roadside one to a catalogue with none or with the random one.
- `CityLayoutDefinition` (`CityDefinition.Layout`) is a city's grid: size, forbidden cells, starting houses with their place. `TryCreateGrid` gives the grid; a city without a usable layout gets a market without grid and one warning at the start. `WorldEconomy.GridOf` returns the grid.
- With a grid, the room of a class at the start is its connected houses on the grid × the room of a house (`EconomyDefinition.CreateMarket`); `StartingRoomOf` is the room a grid should give at least.
- The scene setup gives a city without a layout (`Assets/Data/CityLayouts/<city>Layout.asset`) a 40 by 40 grid (`CityLayoutDefinition.DefaultSize`) with a town hall near its centre and just the starting houses `StartingRoomOf` asks for, placed along roads by `RoadsidePlacement` with the city's index in the map as seed; a city with a layout is never touched. A layout asset already at that path (a link lost) is linked to the city, never rewritten; the layout window's "Create layout" does the same.

## The city view scene

- `CityView.unity` is loaded additively over the map, while the simulation keeps running in the map's scene. `CityViewLoader` (map scene, `World`) is the only way in and out: the city panel's "Voir la ville" calls its `Open`, the view's "Retour à la carte" its `Close`. Never load or unload the scene another way, or the map stays switched off or reacts under the view.
- Opening clears the map's hover and selections (its panels close) and switches off the map camera's object, `WorldMapInput` and `WorldMapInteraction`: the map would otherwise pick, pan and give orders under the view, since the city view reads the same mouse. Closing switches them back on and selects the city again, whose panel reopens, except when fast forward closed the view or is on when the unload ends: the map is then only watched and nothing is selected, the decision being taken when the unload ends. A request that arrives while the scene loads or unloads is kept, the latest winning, and carried out when it is done.
- Opening also remembers the player's ship that was selected on the map and under way (null otherwise), since the view clears that selection: fast forward started from the view awaits it, through `CityViewLoader.TakeShipUnderWay` (see `time.md`). A normal close forgets it, and so does the end of any unload.
- The view's objects reach the world only through `SimulationServices.Current`; the loader hands itself to `CityViewController.Show(city, loader)`.
- The map's `AudioListener` is on its camera's object, which is off while the view is open; the view's camera carries the only other one.
- The scene has no `Light2D`: every sprite of the view uses the unlit sprite material (`Assets/Art/CityView/CityViewSprite.mat`). Its sprites are on the `CityView` layer (made by the setup), the only one its camera draws: the map's objects stay at the same world positions under it.
- `CityViewInput` is the only reader of the `CityView` action map. Click and PanDrag are both the left button: a press released where it started is a click, one moved past the threshold pans. While `TracesWithLeftButton` is on (the road is placed), the left button raises `TraceStarted`/`TraceMoved`/`TraceEnded` instead, and neither clicks nor pans; `MiddlePan` (the middle button) pans either way. Setting `TracesWithLeftButton` drops the gestures in progress, a trace with `TraceCancelled`. It ignores pointer input over any document of the shared panel, the HUDs included: a press there starts nothing.
- `CityGridView` draws one sprite per cell (all at one sortingOrder, below every building) and one per building, ranked by `BuildingDrawOrder` (the order of `IsoProjection.DrawKeyOf`) and given its rank as sortingOrder; a building placed while the view is open is inserted in its rank, nothing is redrawn every frame. The placeholder sprites (`Assets/Art/CityView/`) follow the projection (the 2×1 block's long side goes to the upper right) and have their pivot at the centre of the footprint on the ground, where `IsoProjection.CenterOf(origin, size)` puts them; a footprint size other than 2×1, 1×2, 2×2, 3×2 and 2×3 has no block. Roads are flat cell tiles at sortingOrder 1 (above the ground, below every building, not in `BuildingDrawOrder`); a house or workshop that is not connected is darkened (`inactiveShade`) until `BuildingConnected`; the ghost has three states (`GhostState`: green connected, yellow unconnected, red impossible) and `ShowRoadGhost` draws a road's cells. A house's block takes its class's colour from `HouseColors` (Game), which the layout window also uses, so that both show the same; a workshop's takes the workshop colour of `CityGridView`, whoever owns it. A building's block is scaled down around that pivot (`buildingScale`), which leaves a gap between neighbours and never changes the drawing order; the ghost is scaled the same, so that it shows the building as it will stand.

## Building in the view

- `BuildBarController` (plain class over the view's document) shows the offers of `WorldEconomy.HouseOffers`/`WorkshopOffers`, the only offers that carry their footprint (one made elsewhere is 0 by 0, which `CityPlacementController` refuses), and in its "Routes" category the one `RoadOffer`, quoted for one cell (`PlayerBuilder.QuoteRoadCell`), its cost tip per cell. It quotes through `PlayerBuilder`, and refreshes on the market's, the treasury's and the hold's `Changed` and on `PlayerBuilder.FreeChanged` (the debug tools' free building, during which the cost tip says so), re-choosing the hold (`PlayerBuilder.HoldIn`) when a ship docks in or leaves the city; it is unbound when the view's controller is disabled.
- The workshops category lists only the workshops of the goods the city produces (`MaxWorkersOf` above 0): one of another good could never be built there, so it is left out rather than disabled.
- An offer's button is wrapped in an area that takes the pointer for it: a disabled button gets no pointer event, and its cost tip must still show.
- `CityPlacementController` is the only listener of `CityViewInput.Clicked`, `Rotated`, `Cancelled` and the trace events. Placing the road turns `TracesWithLeftButton` on, and `Cancel` or placing a house or a workshop turns it off; the ghost is one cell until a press, then the L of `RoadTrace.Of` from the cell pressed to the cell under the pointer; dragged off the grid or over the UI, the trace's ghost is kept on the last cell of the grid the pointer was on, not hidden, and a press off the grid or over the UI starts no trace; a release over the UI ends the trace and builds nothing (a release off the grid, not over the UI, lays the trace clamped to the last grid cell); the release lays it through `PlayerBuilder.TryBuildRoads` (one notification per trace) and the road stays placed. A road ghost is red when `QuoteRoads` is impossible, green when a cell is a network road or `JoinsNetwork`, yellow otherwise; a house's or workshop's is green when `WouldConnect`, yellow otherwise. Its `Hover`, `Click`, `TraceStart`, `TraceMove`, `TraceEnd`, `Rotate` and `Cancel` are public so that a play-mode check can drive it without Input System events; `Update` hovers the real pointer every frame while placing, so a driven hover lasts until the next frame.
- The ghost (`CityGridView.ShowGhost`) is drawn at the highest sortingOrder, above every building; it is hidden over the UI and off the grid, and red where the footprint does not fit, partly off the grid included, or wherever the offer's quote (`PlayerBuilder.QuoteHouse`/`QuoteWorkshop`) is impossible; otherwise green if the building would be connected, yellow if not (it can still be built). The bar raises `Refreshed` after each re-quote; `CityViewController` hands it to `CityPlacementController.Refresh`, which stops placing when the offer can no longer be paid for (gold or materials): the player sees its button disabled and the ghost gone, rather than clicks that silently do nothing.
